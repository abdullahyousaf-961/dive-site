DIVE

The football imposter party game. Pass one phone around, give one-word clues, and find who's diving.

How to play

  1. Pass the phone. Everyone sees the same word, except the diver.
  2. The diver gets a similar word, and may not know they're diving.
  3. Take turns giving a one-word clue, then vote for the diver.
  4. Catch the diver and the squad scores. Miss and the divers do.

Help

No sound? Check the speaker button on Home and your phone's silent switch.

Can't kick off? You need at least 3 players with names.

Words repeating in your own category? Its tile shows how many are left. Add more pairs, or tap Reset in Your own to start it over.
