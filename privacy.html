Privacy policy

Dive, the football imposter party game. Effective 12 November 2026.

Dive collects no personal data. Nothing you type or do in the app is sent to us, or to any server.

What the app stores

Dive saves the player names you type, any categories and words you write yourself, and your match settings (categories, number of divers, half length, hints, VAR chaos, sound on or off). They are kept only on your phone, so the next game starts where you left off. Deleting the app deletes them.

Multi phone games

When you host or join a multi phone game, your player name, whether you're ready and your vote go straight to the other phones in the game, over your Wi-Fi or a phone's hotspot. Each phone is sent only its own card. None of this goes over the internet or to us, and nothing is kept once the game ends.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No internet: the app never sends data over the internet. It only talks to other phones on the same Wi-Fi, when you play multi phone.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or microphone. The camera is used only while you scan a host's QR code to join a game; nothing it sees is saved or sent.

Children

Dive collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
