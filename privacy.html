Privacy policy

Dive, the football imposter party game. Effective 11 November 2026.

Dive collects no personal data. Nothing you type or do in the app leaves your phone.

What the app stores

Dive saves the player names you type, any categories and words you write yourself, and your match settings (categories, number of divers, half length, hints, VAR chaos, sound on or off). They are kept only on your phone, so the next game starts where you left off. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No internet connection: the app never sends or receives data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Dive collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
